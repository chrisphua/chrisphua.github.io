Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy describes how MyPlaylist ("the App") handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Data We Collect

MyPlaylist does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our servers. All audio files and playlists remain on your device.

The App uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), which may collect certain device identifiers and usage data to serve advertisements. This data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

2. Advertising

MyPlaylist displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to show relevant ads and measure ad performance.

Where the law requires consent (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the App asks for it before showing ads, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy Choices in the App.

You can opt out of personalised ads at any time via iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

How Google uses information from apps that show its ads: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

If you purchase "Remove Ads", no advertisements are shown, and from then on the App does not start AdMob at all, so no advertising data is collected.

3. App Tracking Transparency

On iOS 14 and later, MyPlaylist will ask for your permission before accessing your device's advertising identifier. You can change your preference at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → MyPlaylist.

4. In-App Purchases

Purchase transactions are handled entirely by Apple. MyPlaylist does not have access to your payment information. Apple's Privacy Policy applies to all transactions.

5. Local Storage

Your library tracks and playlists are stored locally on your device using Apple's standard storage APIs (UserDefaults and the Documents directory). This data is not shared with any third party.

6. iCloud

MyPlaylist supports importing files from iCloud Drive. File access is performed locally on your device; the App does not upload or transmit your audio files anywhere.

7. Children's Privacy

MyPlaylist is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, open an issue.
