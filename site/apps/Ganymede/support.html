Ganymede Synth Support

A polyphonic synthesizer you learn by playing, for iPad, Mac and Android tablets. Found a problem, or have a question? Open an issue, and say which device you use, and which MIDI keyboard if any.

Common questions

My MIDI keyboard does nothing.
Ganymede works with class-compliant USB MIDI keyboards. Unplug it and plug it in again; Ganymede notices a keyboard within a couple of seconds, and names it at the top of the screen.
How do I delete one of My sounds?
Press and hold its tile (right-click on a Mac), then confirm.
Where are my recordings?
After recording, use Share to save or send the file. On iPad they are also in the Files app, under Ganymede › Recordings.

Privacy

Ganymede collects nothing and never connects to the internet. Read the privacy policy.
