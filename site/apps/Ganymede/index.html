GANYMEDE Synth

A synthesizer you learn by playing.

iPad · Mac · Android phones and tablets

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

Start in seconds

  • Sixteen ready-made sounds — brass, strings, pads, leads, basses, keys and bells.
  • Eight big knobs with plain names: Brightness, Bite, Sweep, Fade in, Fade out, Thickness, Space, Echo.
  • See what you hear — live pictures of the volume and the filter as you turn.
  • Surprise me rolls a brand-new sound that always stays playable.

Go deeper

  • The full panel: two oscillators with sync and cross-mod, a resonant filter, two envelopes and an LFO.
  • Two sounds at once — layered on every key, or split across the keyboard.
  • Arpeggio and a 16-step sequencer, with swing and tap tempo.
  • Chorus, delay and reverb.

Play it your way

  • Any USB MIDI keyboard — with built-in support for the Akai MPK mini IV.
  • On iPad and Mac, keeps playing in the background.
  • Unlock, one purchase: save your own sounds, record what you play to a 24-bit WAV, and send MIDI out to other synths or your DAW.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. Ganymede collects nothing, and goes online only when you buy or restore the Unlock. Read the privacy policy.
