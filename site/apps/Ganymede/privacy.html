Privacy Policy

Ganymede Synth · Last updated 24 October 2026

Ganymede collects nothing, and nothing leaves your device. Ganymede is a music app made by Chris Phua. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting, and it never connects to the internet.

What stays on your device

Ganymede keeps a few files in its own private storage on your device:

  • the sounds you save;
  • your arpeggio and sequencer pattern;
  • which theme you chose, and whether you have seen the tour;
  • the map of your MIDI controller's knobs;
  • your recordings, until you delete them. A recording leaves the device only if you share it yourself.

These files never leave your device, and deleting the app deletes them.

MIDI and audio

Ganymede reads notes and knob movements from MIDI keyboards you connect, so it can play them, and can send the notes you play to MIDI devices and apps you choose. It never uses the microphone.

Children

Ganymede is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.
