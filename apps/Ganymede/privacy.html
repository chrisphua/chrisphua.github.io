Privacy Policy

Ganymede Synth · Last updated 26 November 2026

Ganymede needs no account. From version 1.2, on iPhone, iPad and Android it shows one small banner ad, from Google AdMob; apart from that ad, Ganymede itself collects nothing. Ganymede is a music app made by Chris Phua. The Mac app has no ads and collects nothing.

The banner ad (Google AdMob)

A small banner at the top of the screen is served by Google AdMob, a service of Google. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect from your device an advertising identifier, your IP address and the approximate location it implies, basic device and app information, how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data. How Google uses this is set out in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google's Privacy Policy.

  • Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK) Ganymede asks for your consent before any ad is requested.
  • On iPhone and iPad, ads use your advertising identifier only if you allow it when Apple asks; you can change this any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings › Privacy › Ads.
  • The one-time Remove Ads purchase turns the banner, and the ad service, off for good.

Remove Ads

Everything in Ganymede is free. The optional one-time Remove Ads purchase is made through the App Store or Google Play, which handle the payment under their own privacy policies. Ganymede never sees your payment details: it learns only whether Remove Ads is owned, and remembers that on your device.

Piano downloads

The pianos download from GitHub (github.com) the first time you play them. GitHub receives the request, including your IP address, under GitHub's privacy statement. Nothing about you is sent with it.

What stays on your device

Ganymede keeps a few files in its own private storage on your device:

  • the sounds you save, and the pianos you download;
  • your arpeggio and sequencer pattern;
  • which theme you chose, and whether you have seen the tour;
  • the map of your MIDI controller's knobs;
  • your recordings, until you delete them. A recording leaves the device only if you share it yourself.

These files never leave your device, and deleting the app deletes them.

MIDI and audio

Ganymede reads notes and knob movements from MIDI keyboards you connect, so it can play them, and can send the notes you play to MIDI devices and apps you choose. It never uses the microphone.

Children

Ganymede is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13, and its ads are not targeted at children.
