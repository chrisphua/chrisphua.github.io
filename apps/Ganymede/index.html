Help bring Ganymede to Android

Ganymede needs a few more Android testers before it can launch on Google Play. Everything in it is free: every sound, the pianos, saving your own sounds, recording and MIDI out. The beta shows test ads only. Already out on the App Store for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Takes about a minute, on any Android phone or tablet:

  1. Join the tester group with the Google account on your phone (tap Join group; members cannot see each other).
  2. Join the beta with the same account: tap Become a tester.
  3. Install Ganymede from Google Play. It can take a few minutes to appear.
1 · Join the group 2 · Join the beta 3 · Install from Google Play

Then play with it now and then over the two weeks. Anything confusing, broken or lovely? Tap Send feedback in the app's menu, or tell us here.

Start in seconds

  • Over thirty ready-made sounds — brass, strings, pads, leads, basses, keys and bells, all in one list you arrange yourself.
  • Eight big knobs with plain names: Brightness, Bite, Sweep, Fade in, Fade out, Thickness, Space, Echo.
  • See what you hear — live pictures of the volume and the filter as you turn.
  • Surprise me rolls a brand-new sound that always stays playable.

Pianos and worship

  • Four pianos, free to download — a grand, a studio grand, an upright, and Stage grand, voiced to cut through a band.
  • A worship pack — warm pads, slow swells, an ambient drone to hold under a set, choir and strings.
  • Shimmer reverb — the glowing halo an octave above, behind worship pads.

Go deeper

  • The full panel: two oscillators with sync and cross-mod, a resonant filter, two envelopes and an LFO.
  • Two sounds at once — layered on every key, or split across the keyboard.
  • Arpeggio and a 16-step sequencer, with swing and tap tempo.
  • Chorus, delay and reverb.

Play it your way

  • Any USB MIDI keyboard — with built-in support for the Akai MPK mini IV.
  • On iPhone, iPad and Mac, keeps playing in the background.
  • Everything free: save your own sounds, record what you play to a 24-bit WAV, and send MIDI out to other synths or your DAW.
  • One small banner on iPhone, iPad and Android; one purchase, Remove Ads, takes it away for good. The Mac has no ads, and an optional tip if you'd like to support Ganymede.

Privacy

No account. On iPhone, iPad and Android the banner comes from Google AdMob, which asks for your consent where the law requires it and uses an advertising identifier only if you allow it; Remove Ads turns it off. Apart from that ad, Ganymede collects nothing, and the Mac app collects nothing at all. Pianos download from GitHub the first time you play them. Read the privacy policy.
