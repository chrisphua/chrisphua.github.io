MyPlaylist Support

An offline music player for your own files, on iPhone and iPad. Found a problem, or have a question? Open an issue, and say which device and iOS version you use.

Common questions

How do I add my music?
Tap Import Songs or Import a Folder on the Playlist tab (or + in Library), then pick files from the Files app or iCloud Drive. A folder becomes a playlist with its songs in name order.
Can I send a song from another app?
Yes. In Files, AirDrop, Safari or a chat app, share an audio file and choose MyPlaylist. If it is not in the row of apps, tap More at the end.
Which formats play?
MP3, AAC, M4A, WAV, AIFF, FLAC, OPUS, CAF, and the sound of MP4 and other video files.
A file from iCloud Drive will not import.
Wait until it has finished downloading in the Files app (no cloud icon), then try again.
Do I need the internet?
No. Imported songs are stored on your device and play offline. Ads, when shown, need a connection; without one there are simply no ads.
I bought Remove Ads. How do I get it on another device?
Open Settings in MyPlaylist and choose Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID.
