Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

A focused music player built for people who own their music.

📂

Playlist Management

Create, rename, reorder and delete playlists. Bulk-select tracks to add or remove across playlists in one tap.

🖼

Album Art

Artwork is read automatically from ID3 and iTunes tags — shown in the library, mini player, Now Playing screen, and lock screen.

🎛

Audio Visualiser

A real-time pulsing visualiser with radial lines that react to the tempo and amplitude of your music.

🔁

Playback Modes

Sequential, Repeat One, Repeat All, and Shuffle — switch modes instantly from the Now Playing screen.

⚡

Playback Speed

Listen at 0.5× to 2× speed in six steps. Great for podcasts and lectures. Your preferred speed is remembered.

🌙

Sleep Timer

Set a timer for 15, 30, 45, or 60 minutes. Playback stops automatically — a live countdown shows time remaining.

📵

Fully Offline

All imported files are stored locally on your device. No internet connection ever required.

🌗

Appearance Modes

Choose Dark, Light, or follow your system setting — with a clean UI that looks great in all modes.

🌍

10 Languages

Fully localised in English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, and Arabic.

Format Support

Plays everything.
