Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

MyDownloader is a desktop app for macOS and Windows made by Chris Phua. In short: it collects nothing about you, and there is no server of ours for anything to go to.

What MyDownloader collects

Nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. No identifier is assigned to you, and nothing about how you use the app is recorded or sent anywhere.

What the app connects to

MyDownloader runs entirely on your computer. It makes only these connections:

  • The site of the link you paste. To download a video or its audio, the bundled downloader (yt-dlp) connects directly from your computer to that site — YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and so on. That site sees the request as it would from your browser, under its own privacy policy.
  • yt-dlp updates. When the app starts, and when you choose Check for updates, it asks yt-dlp's own release page on GitHub for a newer version of the downloader, so that sites that change still work. GitHub's privacy policy applies to that request.

None of these connections pass through us.

What stays on your computer

The files you download are saved where you choose in the Save dialog. The app also keeps its updated copy of yt-dlp in its own application-data folder. Nothing else is stored, and uninstalling the app removes it.

Children

MyDownloader collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
